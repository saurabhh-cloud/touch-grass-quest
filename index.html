🌿 Touch Grass Quest

An AI adventure planner that gets you off the screen. Tell it where you are and how much time you have, and it plans a short walk, gives you things to look for, and writes your Adventure Journal afterwards. The AI is an open-weight model (Gemma) that runs inside your browser: no account, no server, your location and notes stay on your device.

📱 You seem to be on a phone. The AI planner needs a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge. Plan on a laptop, then tap Download offline card and open it on your phone for the walk. You can still try the sample plan here.
